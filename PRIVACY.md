# MMPlayer Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MMPlayer ("the App", "we", "our") handles user information.

Our philosophy is simple: **MMPlayer is a local, offline music player. Your music, your listening habits, and your data belong solely and exclusively to you.**

## 1. Information Collection and Use

MMPlayer **does not** collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal information, usage data, or analytics with us or any third parties. 

All information generated by using the App remains strictly local on your device. This includes, but is not limited to:
* Your music library (song titles, artists, albums, and cover art).
* Your custom playlists.
* Your custom created tags.
* Your playback history and listening statistics.
* Your preferences and settings within the App.

## 2. Requested Permissions

In order for MMPlayer to function and fulfill its purpose as a local music player, it requires access to certain device permissions:

* **Storage / Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** This permission is strictly necessary for the App to scan your device's folders, find your audio files (MP3, FLAC, etc.), and play them. MMPlayer only accesses media files and does not modify, delete, or read files outside your music library without your explicit consent (for example, when applying a custom cover art).

## 3. Internet Connection

MMPlayer is designed to be an "offline-first" player. The App does not require an internet connection to function, nor does it make background connections to send telemetry, download advertisements, or track your activity.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

MMPlayer is a completely ad-free application. We do not integrate advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or similar) that could compromise your privacy.

## 5. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored exclusively on your device, you have absolute control over it:
* **In-App Deletion:** MMPlayer includes an option in the Settings menu ("Full data wipe") that allows you to instantly clear the internal database, history, cached covers, and settings.
* **Uninstallation:** By uninstalling the App from your device, the operating system will automatically delete all local data and databases generated by MMPlayer.

## 6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or for legal and regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
